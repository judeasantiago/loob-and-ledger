The full study is on the [study page](/study). This post is the short, friendly version.

## The design

The study is quantitative and correlational: researchers do not change anything, they measure two things and look at how they relate. That is why the study cannot say one causes the other.

## The people

The respondents are Filipino youth aged 18–25, college students at the University of Caloocan City, Congress Campus, in academic year 2026–2027. Final details about the sample are still being settled.

## The questionnaire

A researcher-made questionnaire has three parts: a demographic profile, 15 items about utang na loob, and 15 items about family obligation. Each item is answered on a five-point scale from "lubos na hindi sumasang-ayon" (strongly disagree) to "lubos na sumasang-ayon" (strongly agree). It is written in Filipino and built using the earlier work cited in the proposal, including studies of utang na loob among younger Filipinos [@lim2022; @abogadie2025].

## The statistics

- Frequency and percentage describe who answered.
- Mean and standard deviation summarize each variable. Mean scores are read on a scale from "napakababa" (very low) to "napakataas" (very high).
- One-way ANOVA checks for differences by age and educational level.
- Pearson correlation answers the main question: is there a significant relationship between the two?

## The hypotheses

The null says there is no significant relationship. The alternative says there is. A third, directional hypothesis says higher utang na loob goes with higher obligation. The study will test these, and we will report whatever it finds.
