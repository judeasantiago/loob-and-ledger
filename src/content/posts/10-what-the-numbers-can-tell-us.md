## How to read a correlation

A Pearson correlation gives a number between −1 and +1. Near +1 means the two measures rise together, near −1 means one rises as the other falls, and near 0 means no clear linear relationship. A "significant" result means the pattern is unlikely to be chance alone, not that it is large or important.

## What it cannot say

- It cannot say that gratitude causes duty, or the reverse.
- It cannot speak for people outside the sample.
- It depends on honest and thoughtful answers, which the researchers cannot fully control.

## Even a strong result is a beginning

Work such as Ma and colleagues, linking gratitude to filial responsibility through mediating factors [@ma2026], suggests the relationship may involve other variables. Those would be the next questions, for the team or for others.
